Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our team and how this site was built.

Who are we?

We are a two-person CSC 216 team. We built this site together using pair programming, taking turns as driver and navigator.

What is this site built with?

Plain HTML, one CSS file and one JavaScript file. There are no frameworks and no templates.

Why does the site have a dark mode?

Some people find dark screens easier on the eyes. The site follows your system setting at first, and remembers your choice if you change it.

How do we work together on the code?

We share one GitHub repository, commit after every part of the lab, and credit each other as co-authors on commits.

Where is the contact form sent?

Nowhere yet. It checks your input in the browser and shows a confirmation. A real server will be connected in a later lab.
